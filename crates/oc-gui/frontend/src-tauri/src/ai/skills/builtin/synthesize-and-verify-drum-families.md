Use supported oscillator, noise, filter and envelope paths before adding effects.
Read translate-a-production-technique and inspect exact units and support status.
Keep a fixed rhythmic pattern and make one component change per comparison.
Do not use an imported preset name as proof of its synthesis or media dependencies.

## Give each family a controllable source
For a kick separate the initial pitch drop from the body decay and output level.
Tune the stable body against the arrangement; not every short click has a useful note.
For a snare compare a pitched body with a separately shaped noisy tail.
For a clap compare a few close noise bursts with one longer diffuse release.
For a hat use bright, short material and compare closed versus open decay.
Do not assume noise through a filter reproduces a physical cymbal resonator.
If a recipe needs unsupported FM or tuned combs, use a verified external print.
Keep excitation, body and room separate so one can be changed without the others.

## Tune articulation in context
Compare attack, body and tail alone briefly, then restore the complete hit.
Set release from the next event and desired groove, not from a fixed genre number.
Check a quiet and strong hit for velocity-to-level and velocity-to-timbre behavior.
Use deliberate variation; randomizing every control can obscure a stable identity.
For layered hits, check timing, polarity and low-body interaction at several notes.
A visually aligned onset does not guarantee the best audible combination.
Lower drive before interpreting a sudden high-frequency click as useful punch.

## Verify and keep the result
Hear isolated hits, a roll and the intended kick/bass or snare/backbeat phrase.
Compare processed and dry hits at matched perceived level.
Inspect peak headroom and listen for alias-like grit or tails cut by voice stealing.
Check mono compatibility of widened noisy layers while retaining central body.
If printing, keep the editable patch, source note and sufficiently long tail.
Map the accepted print with assemble-a-sampled-drum-kit and verify choke behavior.
Stop when the hit serves the phrase; more layers are not an acceptance criterion.
