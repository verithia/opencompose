Name the job before searching: a clear attack, stable low body, pitched answer or moving texture.
Listen to the gap it must fill and the sound immediately before and after it.
Choose two or three candidates from approved media or a small sample_search result.
Search by audible traits such as a dry woody hit or a short resonant bass stab.
Check availability and quality; use sample_pull for an approved result and respect licence_notice.
Put a downloaded media_path in its own audio clip before requesting its clip index.
Use audio_clips_list and audition_clip_source to hear the attack, body and complete decay.

## Fit the gesture, then judge the source
Put each candidate in the same musical slot and compensate its playback level.
Keep the surrounding notes, routing and effects fixed for this first comparison.
Refresh the clip index and revision before playback or range edits.
Find the useful attack inside the source before moving the written onset to hide a slow start.
For a pitched hit, hear its stable body against the bass or chord; a name or key tag is only a clue.
For a slice bank, verify the receiving region before treating its trigger key as an audible pitch.
Test the phrase's lowest and highest notes. If retuning erases the body, choose a closer source register.
For a loop, identify a pickup and a later accent before stretching; read align-and-stretch-audio.
Keep pitch, timing and formant decisions separate when the source needs independent changes.

## Control what happens between hits
Save a source-only alternative before changing pitch or occupied duration.
Compare pitch-only and tail-only alternatives before combining the changes.
If the replacement covers the next kick or syllable, shorten its envelope or clip with a clean fade.
If a useful decay carries the groove, preserve it and try moving or thinning the competing part.
If the front clicks, hear the cut and fade before dulling the entire sound with EQ.
If it flams or sounds hollow, check duplicate paths and source attack alignment before changing the groove.
A processed source may already contain space or distortion; hear it before adding those again.

## Keep the better musical fit
Use render_range for the same phrase and tail, then audio_compare with listen: true and listening_only: true.
Use corresponding short passages for matched listening; audition the complete phrase separately.
Hear the candidate alone, with its nearest competitor, and in the full arrangement over two repeats.
Choose by the required attack, pitch support and useful gaps, rather than extra solo loudness.
If none fits, change the search description or the arrangement instead of stacking repairs indefinitely.
Validate the chosen edit and keep its source recoverable with the project.
