id: program-phonk-cowbell-and-bass
title: Program phonk cowbell and bass
when: A drift phonk or Memphis-influenced beat needs a tuned metallic hook and 808 with clear attacks under drive.
category: production
version: 1
tools: [devices_catalog, presets_list, presets_set_params, project_extract, edit_subtree, render_range, audio_analyze, audition_range]
---
Identify the intended cowbell note against a sustained tuned reference.
A metallic sample can have several strong peaks. Confirm the pitch the
ear follows before setting the sampler root. Audition the hook across
the notes it actually uses, especially at the edges of its range.

Keep the hook and bass on independent tracks. Set the low note and its
length without distortion first. On a glide, verify the target pitch
and whether the first note after a gap restarts cleanly.

Add drive in a controlled pass and trim output for comparison. A quiet
note may become more audible as harmonics grow, while a loud note can
lose contrast as the peaks flatten. Keep enough difference for the
groove. Avoid raising drive and listening level together.

Use velocity changes to emphasize the melody. If the instrument responds
with both brightness and level, tune them separately where supported.
Choose a shorter decay if repeat notes blur together.

Check the full loop for a clear kick attack, a legible hook and stable
low notes. Compare mono and stereo and include the busiest phrase.
