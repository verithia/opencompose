Inventory what the print already contains before adding layers. A full
drum loop may contain kick, snare, bass, stereo room and limiting. A top loop
may still contain a clap or pitched tone. Use the ear, not its folder label.
Keep the untouched source and hear it with audition_clip_source.

## Assign one owner to each role
Decide whether the loop owns the groove or supplies texture. Program new
parts around its accents. Duplicate kicks or basses only for a defined attack,
body or motion role. Test muting each layer before trying corrective EQ.
If a layer competes throughout, choose a sparser source or remove a part.
A high-pass can reduce lows but does not turn a full mix into separated stems.

Check whether printed reverb, saturation or compression is being repeated
on its track, group or master. A dry control must actually exclude baked FX;
turning off current inserts does not remove effects inside the file.
For a live or synthesized voice, inspect instrument and per-part effects too.
Separate these from track inserts and shared returns with controlled bypasses.
If a stage is unavailable or already printed, keep its contribution uncertain.
Maintain the original routing when comparing one change. Avoid normalizing
every source independently and then calling the resulting balance preserved.

## Keep the pocket
Hear several shared hits and gaps at the final tempo. Use placement for a
consistent offset and sparse warp for drift; preserve intentional swing.
Do not align the first peaks of different sounds blindly. Compare attack
and body separately with align-layered-transients when low weight disappears.
For kick/bass overlap, first try source length, note gaps or complementary
sounds. Read unmask-kick-and-bass before using broadband ducking.

Render a dry/reference balance and the proposed balance at the same range.
Hear quiet events, dense hits and the fill into the next phrase. Listen for
flams, pumping, unnatural cut tails and a loop that overpowers the lead.
Keep a layer only when its function remains useful after loudness matching.
Validate, retain source media and state any remaining baked-processing limit.
