Name the intended center and spell the actual pitch collection before editing.
Sharing seven pitches does not make two passages share the same tonic.
The first chord, key label and final melody note are clues, not proof alone.

## Establish the center
Keep one short phrase, sound and playback level fixed for the comparison.
Test a bass pedal, longer tonic duration or repeated return to the intended
home chord. Use only as much reinforcement as the passage needs.
Hear where the melody comes to rest and how the next phrase begins.
Compare the same notes with a different bass or cadence to hear the center
change without pretending that every written pitch has changed scale.

## Make the characteristic degree audible
Compare A minor with F natural against A Dorian with F sharp.
Keep other notes, timing and register fixed for this first test.
Check the F sharp against the sounding chord, bass and held melody.
A D-major chord can expose it in A Dorian, but that chord is not mandatory.
Put the characteristic pitch in a useful melodic, inner or bass role;
repeating the scale upward is not the same as composing a modal phrase.
Avoid assigning one guaranteed emotion to a mode or counting happy chords.

## Borrow deliberately
Choose the destination and voice movement before adding an altered chord.
For a local E-major or E7 arrival toward A minor, inspect G sharp as the
leading tone and recheck any G natural sustained by another part.
Both pitches can coexist intentionally; judge the actual register and rhythm.
Distinguish one temporary tonicization from a section that establishes a new key.
Keep degree numbering explicit when switching between minor-tonic and
relative-major analysis. Read writing-notes for the resulting note syntax.

## Verify the transition
Validate the edited phrase, then audition its lead-in, altered event and return.
Compare a simpler diatonic version at the same sound and level.
Hear whether the intended center remains clear and the melody's sustained
notes retain their role. Preserve ambiguity when the user wants it.
Record uncertain inner pitches as uncertain rather than infer a complex mode
from one spectral peak or a suggested scale highlight.
