---
id: design-hard-dance-kicks
title: Design pitched hard dance kicks
when: A hardstyle, raw hardstyle or gabber kick needs a stable attack, pitched tail, or controlled distortion.
category: production
version: 1
tools: [devices_catalog, presets_list, presets_duplicate, presets_set_params, project_extract, edit_subtree, render_range, audition_range, audio_analyze]
---
Split the job into attack, body and pitched tail. Keep a repeatable attack
that can be auditioned on its own. Tune the stable part of the tail against
the bass or root, after the initial pitch fall. Tune by the actual waveform
or by ear when distortion makes the strongest peak a harmonic.

Use one source for the attack and a second for the pitched tail if mapping
one sample moves the attack too far. Start the tail where it joins the body.
Try a short overlap and compare polarity and timing across several notes.
Check exact envelope and tuning controls in devices_catalog.

Add drive a stage at a time. Listen for the pitch becoming unclear or the
attack turning into a brittle click. Trim level after drive for fair checks.
Print a few versions if the best tail depends on note, and keep the editable
sources. Check that printed start points and note mapping stay consistent.

Program reverse bass or kick rolls around the attack. Use a deliberate gap
before the drop when impact depends on silence, and audition any effect tail
that crosses it. Compare the kicks in the actual sequence at matched level.

Check the deepest and highest tail notes in mono and confirm headroom with
the lead and returns active. State which part of the kick was retuned.
