id: shape-transients-and-decay
title: Shape transients and decay
when: A drum, pluck or texture needs more attack, less sustain, or control of overly sharp hits.
category: mixing
version: 1
tools: [devices_catalog, project_extract, effects_add, effects_set_param, render_range, audio_analyze, audition_range]
---
Name the missing cue. The start may need definition, a peak may be too
sharp, or the tail may be too long for the next note. Listen to isolated
hits, then the phrase, to tell these apart.

For a sampler or synth, adjust the source envelope or select another
sample before adding bus processing. Shorten decay or release when notes
run together. Retain enough length to hear the instrument and pitch.
If only a few hits are wrong, adjust those hits first.

When processing audio, choose a device the catalog actually offers.
A compressor with a slower attack can let the front through. A faster
attack can catch the peak and also remove the cue that locates the hit.
Set release with the rhythm and verify recovery before the next event.

A more prominent front can be achieved by reducing the tail instead of
raising peak level. Avoid pushing the downstream limiter harder just to
hear the change. Check low drums for distortion and bright hits for clicks.

At matched playback level, compare attack shape, space between notes and
level. Use peak and crest as supporting evidence. Keep the edit only if
the result retains its intended character in the arrangement.
