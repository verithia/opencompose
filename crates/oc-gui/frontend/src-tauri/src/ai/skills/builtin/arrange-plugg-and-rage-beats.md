id: arrange-plugg-and-rage-beats
title: Arrange plugg and rage rap beats
when: A plugg, PluggnB, rage rap or cloud rap beat needs a clearer hook, space for the voice, or more defined bass accents.
category: arrangement
version: 1
tools: [project_extract, tracks_list, edit_subtree, presets_list, presets_set_params, devices_catalog, audition_range]
---
Start from a reference phrase for the selected scene. Decide which layer
carries the melodic hook and where the rap phrasing has room to enter.

For plugg, shorten a bell decay if the next snare disappears. Move the
countermelody up or down an octave if it crowds the vocal. Try moving a
few hat hits around a fixed kick before shifting the whole groove.

For PluggnB, keep the useful upper chord color and remove a doubled low
chord tone when the 808 already carries it. Leave space for a sustained
vocal glide and treat the tail of a phrase separately from its entrance.

For rage rap, protect the top note of the saw hook while softening harsh
brightness. Give repeated chords a short release gap before key downbeats.
If a synth and bass share drive, route the kick separately when its attack
changes the amount of saturation on those parts.

For cloud rap, let one atmospheric layer carry the long tail. Keep its
low end and phrase timing clear of the kick and sub. Shorten individual
bass notes where they obscure the rhythm.

Audition the loop with a voice or a deliberate space for the voice.
Compare two neighboring sections and check the first word after a break.
