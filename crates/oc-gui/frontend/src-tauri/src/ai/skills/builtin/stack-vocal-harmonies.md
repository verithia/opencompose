Name each backing role: unison texture, chord voice, held vowel, response or
ad-lib. Place one useful entry before filling the section. A strong lead can
need no stack; track count is not a finish criterion.

## Write the voices against the song
Hear bass, sounding chords and lead. A constant semitone transpose differs
from a diatonic third, and an in-scale note can still clash with this chord.
Compare parallel motion with a held common tone or independent answering line.
A pedal may be low or high; check its tension and resolution as chords change.
For close versus open spacing, keep chord identity while moving voices by
octaves. Check register and sung range, not just a wide root-to-top interval.
Read voice-chords-around-bass-and-melody for uncertain inner notes or extensions.
Let the lead carry words while a background vowel carries sustain; avoid
stacking every breath and consonant into a second attack. Give answers a gap.

## Distinguish performances from processed copies
Use independent takes when available. An identical aligned copy only changes
level; an offset copy can create comb filtering or an audible late syllable.
A derived voice can be a useful effect, but is not an independent performance.
Retain source and compare pitch/formant processing on vowels, transitions and
consonants. Do not promise separate vibrato control from a whole-clip shift.
Pan and blend according to the role. Four takes, paired takes, hard panning
and identical chains are options, not universal requirements.
A group compressor responds to the sum differently from per-take inserts.
Do not feed several simultaneous notes into a monophonic tuner by default.

## Sketch a derived harmony
An @vocal_harmony block derives one voice per source clip. Use a monophonic
lead and confirm the active key. Its interval counts diatonic steps in the
active scale; missing scale produces a warning and semitone fallback.
Check its notes against actual chord changes and passing tones, not key alone.
Use a separate recorded or programmed line where the sketch lands awkwardly.
The block's level and pan blend that voice on the lead track; inspect the
actual generated behavior before describing an independent backing track.

Audition lead and backs over their entry, full phrase and final cutoff.
Check loud/quiet vowels, intelligibility and balance in mono and stereo.
Reduce density, shorten backing sustain or darken its role before repeatedly
raising the lead. Validate edits and preserve the intended lead identity.
