---
id: program-clave-and-montuno
title: Program clave and piano montuno
when: A salsa arrangement needs coherent clave direction, interlocking piano and bass, or percussion that supports the vocal.
category: arrangement
version: 1
tools: [project_extract, project_get, tracks_list, tracks_create, edit_subtree, project_validate, audition_range]
---
Identify the reference's two-bar clave cycle before writing the parts.
Count which side has three strokes and which has two. Keep the cycle marked
across section boundaries, including any pickup, extension or break.

Write a small piano montuno that works across both bars. Let its accents
relate to the clave without simply playing every clave stroke. Check where
the bass anticipates the harmony and leave space for that entrance.

Lay down one percussion reference and add conga, bell or other layers
separately. Check the register and stroke emphasis of the available kit.
Leave contrasting accents in the less busy gaps instead of doubling each
stroke with every part.

Audition the two-bar loop with bass and piano only. Add percussion and
listen again with the lead or vocal. Confirm that the fill lands into the
expected side of the next cycle. A one-bar edit can flip that relationship.

Write a simpler version under a dense vocal section. Restore the fuller
figure at a planned entrance and compare through the transition rather
than only listening to the loop in isolation.
