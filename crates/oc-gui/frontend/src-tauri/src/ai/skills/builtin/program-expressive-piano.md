Choose the piano's job: foreground melody, rhythmic accompaniment or quiet
harmonic support. Hear the available patch with a soft and a firm note.
A velocity change may select a different recorded attack, not only change level.

## Separate the voices
Keep bass, melody and inner chord tones identifiable in the written notes.
Hold the melody and bass fixed while comparing close and open upper voicings.
Do not add a low root where another instrument already supplies enough weight.
Shape the phrase toward a destination before adding note-by-note variation.
Make the melody speak through selected accents and softer inner voices;
random velocity across every note can obscure that hierarchy.

## Compare textures
Write one short block-chord control and one broken-chord variation.
Keep their harmony, phrase length and overall level comparable.
Choose the order of bass, fifth, inner tone and melody for the intended pulse.
Use explicit positions from writing-notes for rolled attacks and pickups.
A roll does not require every chord to use the same spread or direction.
Leave a rest where it supports the next entrance rather than fill every gap.
Check reachable register and note density when the part represents one player.

## Pedal and release
Hear the dry note release before adding pedal or room effects.
Use written pedal down and pedal up marks when sustained resonance is wanted.
Compare a clean change with repedaling around the next chord attack.
Keep intentional common-tone overlap; clear resonance that obscures a new bass
or melody. A chord boundary is a place to listen, not an automatic full cut.
Inspect the final pedal-up and note releases, including the loop seam.
Live recorded sustain may already be folded into saved note lengths.
Do not double that duration blindly with additional pedal marks.

## Verify the phrase
Validate and check compatibility after the smallest useful edit.
Audition the piano alone, with bass and lead, then in the full section.
Include the pickup, chord change and final decay in the same comparison.
If an accent is missing, inspect its source layer and written velocity before
compressing the whole part. Keep the texture whose musical role remains clear.
