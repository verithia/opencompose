---
id: unmask-kick-and-bass
title: Unmask kick and bass
when: A kick disappears under an 808 or bass, or sidechain pumping weakens the low-end groove.
category: mixing
version: 1
tools: [devices_catalog, tracks_list, effects_add, effects_set_param, render_range, audio_analyze, audition_range]
---
Pick a section with kick and bass both hitting together and separately.
Audition each alone and then the pair. Identify whether the missing cue
is the kick's fundamental, its upper attack, or the bass note's definition.

Try changes in arrangement, note length, sample envelope or level first.
If overlap happens on every hit, leave a brief gap or choose complementary
sounds. If the conflict only happens on some notes, avoid a broad static cut.

For triggered ducking, key a compressor on the bass from the kick. Set the
depth by listening to the overlap, then set recovery so the following bass
note speaks. When only one band masks, try a dynamic EQ band on the bass.
Use the kick as the device sidechain and enable the band's sidechain switch.
Check exact device IDs, parameters and units in devices_catalog.

Compare dry and processed at similar playback level. A quick recovery can
buzz or chatter on a long low cycle. A slow recovery can swallow the next
bass note. Check quiet hits, dense fills, sustained bass and syncopations.

Render the same range and check low-band energy, transients and mono weight.
Keep the setting that clarifies the groove with the smallest audible side
effect. Name the trigger, target, band or depth, and any remaining compromise.
