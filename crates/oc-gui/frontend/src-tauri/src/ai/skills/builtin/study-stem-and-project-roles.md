Choose one passage and a question: what carries the hook, what makes the
bass read, or what changes at the chorus? Keep the supplied material intact.
Read rebuild-a-reference-passage for importing authorized source material.

## Predict, reveal, test
Hear the original first and record a short role inventory without treating
file names as the answer. Mark uncertain layers, registers and entrances.
Inspect actual instruments, trigger maps, source windows, notes and routing.
Keep pitch, onset, duration, velocity and articulation attached to each note.
Do not infer sustained roles from separate pitch and duration ranges.
Verify whether automation overrides saved enable state in the chosen passage.
Check clip, lane, generator and strip activation, then every parent gain to main.
A populated source feeding a zero-gain bus is not evidence of an audible layer.
Keep arrangement regions separate from stored takes, unused patterns and caches.
A frozen or printed tail is not automatically another instrument or performance.
Hear likely roles separately at their existing relative levels.
A label such as strings can conceal a processed texture; classify what it does.
Compare the full passage with one layer omitted, keeping other gains fixed.
Then test a useful combination, such as bass plus lead or lead plus its answer.
Does removing the bed change continuity, room, tension or nothing you can hear?
Keep missing media, opaque settings and unheard layers explicitly uncertain.

## Extract a production decision
Write what the part contributes, when it enters, what it avoids and which
other part it needs. Separate written rhythm from sample onset delay and tail.
Distinguish recorded character, clip edits, instrument processing, inserts and returns.
A dry stem may omit the original space; identical project bytes with different media need not sound alike.
A stem-sum match can check reconstruction; it cannot certify separation quality.
Restore temporary mutes before making or judging a production change.

## Rebuild a bounded passage
Choose independent sources for the same roles, preserving the intended accent,
register and phrase shape before adding processors. Recreate one missing role
at a time rather than copying a plugin list or making every stem louder.
Compare the same passage and note an actual difference: bass length, answer
placement, onset hardness, quiet bed or transition density. Retain the simpler
choice when another layer makes no useful change. Validate edits.
Finally hear the surrounding entrance and ending. A convincing short exercise
does not establish a complete-song match or prove unheard high-frequency detail.
